How it works

1 · Verify

The ov-w3c-btn web component from @credenceid/online-verifier opens a W3C OpenID4VP session, renders a QR code, and polls for the wallet's response. The backend cryptographically validates the credential.

2 · Ingest

The genuine ov-success result — name, birth year, portrait, trust metadata — becomes one bronze record. Nothing is typed, copied, or invented.

3 · Trust

Silver normalizes and dedupes; gold aggregates. The name on the application is checked against the verified ID — mismatches get flagged, not silently accepted.
